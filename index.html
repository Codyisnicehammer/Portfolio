In Progress
The Causal Impact of Managerial Stability on Elite Football Performance
A Synthetic Control Approach to the Post-Ferguson Era

This study transitions from descriptive analysis to causal inference, utilizing the Synthetic Control Method (SCM) to quantify the performance deficit resulting from organizational instability following a major leadership transition. By constructing a weighted counterfactual from a donor pool of comparable European clubs, the research isolates the causal effect of managerial turnover — independent of transfer spending, squad quality, and market conditions.
